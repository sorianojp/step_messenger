## Description (1,632 / 4,000 characters)

Uhoo! is the official messaging app of Universidad de Dagupan. It gives the whole
school one place to talk, separate from the personal chat apps where class
announcements get lost between family photos and group memes.

Sign in with your STEP account. There is no new registration and no new password,
and everyone inside is a verified member of the school community.

ONE APP FOR CAMPUS CONVERSATIONS

Ask a classmate what you missed. Message a teacher about a deadline without asking
around for a number first. Bring an entire section together in a group chat named
after the class, or keep a project team, an office or a student organisation in
their own space.

Send handouts, forms, photos and documents straight into the conversation, and
open them again later without digging through your files.

Read receipts tell you a message landed. Unread counts show what still needs your
attention. Notifications reach you even when the app is closed, and a busy group
can be muted without leaving it.

KNOW WHO YOU ARE TALKING TO

Names, roles and departments come from STEP, so a message from a teacher is
clearly from a teacher. Search brings up people from your own school only. Nobody
from outside the campus can reach you here.

QUIET BY DESIGN

No advertising. No analytics trackers. No third-party marketing. Your messages are
used to run the service and nothing else. Light and dark appearance follow your
device settings.

BEFORE YOU DOWNLOAD

Uhoo! requires a STEP account at Universidad de Dagupan and an internet connection.
It is a school app, not a public messaging service, and cannot be used without a
school account.

## Screenshot requirements

The build targets iPhone and iPad (TARGETED_DEVICE_FAMILY = "1,2"), so Apple
requires both sets.

| Display | Device to capture on | Pixel size (portrait) | Required |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6.9" iPhone | iPhone 17 Pro Max | 1320 × 2868 | Yes |
| 13" iPad | iPad Pro 13-inch (M4) | 2064 × 2752 | Yes, while iPad is targeted |

Apple scales the 6.9" set down for smaller iPhones, so one iPhone set is enough.
Up to 10 screenshots per size. Screenshots must show the iOS app: Android captures
are rejected.
