## Notes for Review

We request public App Store distribution so Universidad de Dagupan students,
faculty, and staff can find and install Uhoo! on their personally owned iPhones
and iPads without a managed-device enrollment or a private installation link.
Anyone can download the app, but messaging requires an existing
university-issued STEP account. There is no public self-registration and no paid
content. We have made this limitation clear in the App Store description.

The Google Play badge, link, and other Android-platform references previously
visible on the STEP authentication webpage have been removed from the iOS
sign-in experience. Please test the complete sign-in flow using the review
account provided in App Store Connect.

The public support page is available at:

https://uhoo.udd.edu.ph/support

## Guideline 3.2 response

1. Yes. The app is restricted to verified students, faculty, and staff of
   Universidad de Dagupan.
2. The app is designed for a single educational organization: Universidad de
   Dagupan. Other companies and organizations cannot become clients or use the
   application.
3. The app has no features intended for the general public. It provides campus
   messaging, class group conversations, file sharing, notifications, and
   communication between verified members of the university.
4. Users receive accounts through the university's existing STEP system. Public
   registration, invitation-based registration, and personal account creation
   are not available.
5. There is no paid content, subscription, in-app purchase, or fee charged to
   users. Universidad de Dagupan provides the service to its authorized users.

We request public App Store distribution because students and staff use their
personally owned devices and need to find and install the app independently.
Downloading the app is open to anyone, while access to campus conversations
requires a STEP account issued by the university. The account restriction is
disclosed in the App Store description.
