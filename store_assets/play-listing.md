## Full description (1,614 / 4,000 characters)

Uhoo! is the official messaging app of Universidad de Dagupan. It keeps
conversations between students, teachers and school staff in one place, separate
from personal chat apps.

Sign in with your STEP account. There is no separate registration, no password to
remember, and only members of the school community can join.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Message anyone in your school — send a direct message to a classmate, teacher
  or staff member.
• Create group chats — organise a class, a project team, an office or a student
  organisation.
• Share files and photos — send handouts, forms, images and documents, and open
  them straight from the conversation.
• Know when messages arrive — read receipts show what has been seen, and unread
  counts show what still needs attention.
• Get notified — push notifications reach you when someone messages you, even
  when the app is closed.
• Mute what can wait — silence a busy group without leaving it.
• Find people quickly — search by name and see each person's role and department.
• Read comfortably — light and dark themes follow your phone's settings.

BUILT FOR SCHOOLS

Your name, role and department come from your STEP account, so you always know who
you are talking to. Uhoo! shows only people from your own school community.

PRIVACY

Uhoo! carries no advertising, no analytics trackers and no third-party marketing.
Your messages are used to run the service and nothing else. Full details:
https://uhoo.udd.edu.ph/privacy

REQUIREMENTS

A STEP account at Universidad de Dagupan and an internet connection. Uhoo! is not
a public messaging app and cannot be used without a school account.
